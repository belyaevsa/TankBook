## What to build

### 1. The switcher sheet
Per the artboard: each car with its **own** vitals (odometer, consumption in that car's units, this
month's spend), archived cars shown but visually distinct, and `Add car`.

**An EV shows `kWh/100`, a petrol car shows `L/100`** – per-vehicle units, not a global setting. The
artboard shows exactly this contrast. Electric figures use `headlight`, fuel uses `taillight`
(hard rule 5: the accents encode powertrain).

### 2. The selected-car invariant – the substance of this task
From the artboard footer: **"Capture always logs to the selected car."**

- The selection **persists across launches**.
- Every entry-creating path (the manual form today, capture later) writes to the **selected** vehicle,
  never to "the first one".
- Home, the log stream, and Trends all read the **same** selection. They must never disagree about which
  car is on screen.

This is the bug class that silently corrupts data: an entry filed against the wrong car is invisible
until someone's consumption looks wrong months later.

### 3. Archived cars (J13)
Archived cars keep their history and stay visible here, marked as archived, but are **out of active
stats**. They must not appear as the selected car by default, and the label carries the honest detail the
artboard shows (`sold Mar 2026 · history kept`).

### 4. The free-tier car limit – the ONE monetization surface
From `docs/ERRORS.md`, verbatim:

| Condition | Shows | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Free-tier car limit reached on "Add car" | Sheet explains the cap (never mid-capture): "Free keeps up to 3 cars. Archive one, or go Pro." | Archive a car · Pro · cancel. Existing cars never locked (anti-CarScope rule) |

Three constraints, all load-bearing:
- **This is the only place monetization may appear** in the entire app (hard rule 5). Nowhere else, and
  never mid-capture.
- **Existing cars are never locked.** Hitting the cap prevents *adding*, it never takes away what the
  user already has. This is the explicit anti-CarScope rule from `docs/COMPETITORS.md`.
- All three next steps must be present and reachable.

Gate the limit behind a flag/constant so it is testable without a real entitlement system (Pro is P6).

## Tests

**L1 (`ios/Tests/TankbookCoreTests/`, macOS, no simulator)**:
- **Entries file against the selected car**: with two vehicles, saving writes to the selected one, and
  switching then saving writes to the other. Assert the persisted `vehicleId`, not UI state.
- **Selection persists** across a store reopen.
- **Per-vehicle stats are independent**: two cars with different histories produce different headlines,
  and one car's entries never contribute to the other's figures.
- **Archived cars are excluded from active stats** but keep their history (J13), and are not selected by
  default.
- **Units are per vehicle**: an EV yields kWh/100, a petrol car L/100, from the same code path.
- **The car-limit rule**: at the cap, adding is refused; **existing cars remain fully usable** –
  readable, editable, entries still saveable. Assert that explicitly, since it is the anti-CarScope
  promise.

**L4 (`ios/App/UITests/`)**:
- The switcher lists cars with their own vitals; the archived row renders distinctly.
- Switching changes Home's garage card and the log stream to the other car's data.
- The limit sheet shows all three next steps and cancelling leaves everything intact.
