## The one invariant this task exists to keep
**No AdBlue litre ever reaches fuel consumption.** It is enforced by `FuelKind.family` (a third
family, `adBlue`), not by a flag on the fill and not by a filter in a view. Before you write the
feature, write the test that interleaves AdBlue fills into the D1-D4 golden datasets and asserts
the vectors are unchanged; then break the family mapping and watch it fail; then build.

## Fences (each exists because something went wrong once)
- Never hand-edit a generated schema JSON; change the Swift model and run the generator. The
  parity test will catch drift, but only if you run it.
- `isFull` on an AdBlue fill is stored and ignored - do not add a branch that reads it.
- The Confirm fuel row offers exactly `vehicle.fuelKinds` (P2.3b rule); a single-kind car shows
  no chooser. Do not special-case AdBlue in the row - it falls out of the offer set.
- The mixed-receipt AdBlue line becomes a `FillUp(.adBlue)` in the purchase group. It must not
  become an `Expense`, and the diesel line must remain the fill even when the AdBlue line's
  litres × price cross-check locks. Fixture-pin both.
- The Trends tile appears at the second AdBlue fill and is absent before - assert absence, not
  just presence.
- Every user-facing string in EN and RU; RU reviewed for case governance
  (`docs/LOCALIZATION.md`). The kind label is `AdBlue` in both languages (a brand name).
- Numbers grouped through `OdometerFormat` / the shared formatters (P1.13, P1.13b).
