## The spec

`docs/SCHEMA.md` -> "AdBlue" (~line 889) is the design, decided 2026-08-30, and the P1.14 row in
`docs/TASKS.md` lists every piece and its checks. Build that, plus the owner's 2026-10-01 placement:

- **`FuelKind.adBlue`**, its own `FuelKind.family` (`combustion / electric / adBlue`), so a segment
  never spans families and no AdBlue litre reaches L/100 km, the headline, the lifetime average, the
  anomaly engine or the D1-D4 vectors. `isFull` is ignored for AdBlue.
- **ADBLUE RATE** = Σ volume of AdBlue fills except the last ÷ (odo(last) − odo(first)), in
  **L / 1000 km**, lifetime, distance-weighted; ≥ 2 AdBlue fills with odometers, else unavailable (`–`),
  never estimated.
- **The car's Home card** gains one quiet line, only for a car that has at least one AdBlue fill:
  e.g. "AdBlue · 5.75 L on 30 Sep · 1.2 L/1000 km" (rate shown as `–` with one fill). Its style is a
  subordinate line, not a second hero figure (`docs/DESIGN.md`: numbers DIN, units subordinate, the
  droplet glyph from the AdBlue row rule). RU as a full phrase, no concatenation.
- **Trends**: the AdBlue tile from the design (rate; add the last fill) shown only with ≥ 2 fills.
- **Money**: AdBlue spend counts wherever fuel spend counts (monthly totals, cost/km), never in litres.
- **Offer set**: `.adBlue` in `Vehicle.fuelKinds` only alongside `.diesel` (CHECK 4, AddVehicle and
  Vehicle detail); Confirm/manual fill-up offers two chips on a `[.diesel, .adBlue]` car.
- **Receipts**: an AdBlue line is never the fuel line; a diesel+AdBlue receipt is a mixed receipt whose
  AdBlue line becomes a second `FillUp(.adBlue)` in the purchase group (`docs/EXTRACTION.md` -> AdBlue).
  A standalone AdBlue receipt (the corpus has `receipt-111` / `receipt-114`, Neste AdBlue 5,75 L x
  0,899) yields one `.adBlue` fill. Keep the receipt corpus marks: `AccuracyRatchetTests` must not drop.
- **Import**: Spritmonitor / MFM AdBlue types map to `.adBlue` where the source says so; never guessed.
- **Log row**: droplet glyph and the kind label (`docs/DESIGN.md` ~190).
