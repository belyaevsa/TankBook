## The core rule of this screen: the third value derives

From `docs/SCHEMA.md`, verbatim:

> `unitPrice` is stored, not derived: receipts print all three numbers and the redundancy IS the
> confidence signal. When the user types only two, the third derives on save and
> `crossCheck = .notApplicable`.

So:

- The three numbers are **total money**, **volume (litres)**, and **price per litre**.
- Type any **two**, the third is derived on save, and `crossCheck` is **`.notApplicable`** – because with
  only two independent values there is no redundancy left to check.
- Type all **three**, and the cross-check applies: `volumeL × unitPrice ≈ money.amount`, tolerance
  `max(0.02, 0.5%)`. Within tolerance → `.verified`. Outside → `.mismatch(field:)`, with the amber
  treatment from ERRORS.md and a check line that **refuses to lock**.
- The artboard's caption for the derived field is `"fills in from total ÷ liters"` and the live hint is
  `"checks as you type"`. Save is disabled until two of the three exist – the artboard's save bar reads
  `"Enter total and liters to save"` in that state.

**`volumeL` is always stored in litres** regardless of the vehicle's display unit (SCHEMA.md).

## What to build

### 1. The ConfirmManual sheet
Per the artboard: the three-number card with live derivation, fuel kind, `isFull` toggle, **odometer**
(artboard: `119 486 km`, caption `"last known · update after typing fuel"`), **station** with a nearby
suggestion, date, and the taillight `Save fill-up` bar.

### 2. Money is a pair (hard rule 3)
Store original currency **and** home currency with a rate snapshot whose `rateDate` is the **entry
date**, never today. There is no rates service yet, so when no rate is available, follow ERRORS.md:
hint `"≈ – · converts when online"`, and **save anyway** – the snapshot backfills later, fill-blanks-only.
Never silently convert. The currency chip row is one tap (ERRORS.md, currency low-confidence).

### 3. Error states from `docs/ERRORS.md` → Confirm, the ones that apply to the manual variant
Use the copy verbatim; do not invent wording.

| Condition | Shows | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-check mismatch (F2) | Amber underline on the suspect field + "these don't multiply up – check the amber field"; check line refuses to lock | Tap field → correct · save anyway (entry flagged) |
| Currency low-confidence | Currency chip amber: "Which currency is this?" – never silently converts | One tap on the chip row |
| No exchange rate for that date (F9) | Hint on conversion card: "≈ – · converts when online" | Save anyway (converts later) · enter rate manually |
| Odometer breaks timeline (F9a) | Amber + the conflicting entry quoted: "Aug 17 already recorded 119 486 km." | Fix odometer (preselected) · fix date (needs explicit override) · save anyway (flagged) |

Rule 7 applies to all of them: **every error names its next step and survives being ignored.** "Save
anyway" always exists and flags the entry rather than blocking. **No monetization on this screen, ever.**

The F9a check must come from the existing validation engine in `TankbookCore/Validation/`, not from new
UI-side logic.
