## What to build

### 1. The Home screen per `HomeA.dc.html`
Garage card (car name, photo, odometer, "updated <date>"), the vitals row, and the recent-entries
section. Every figure comes from the consumption engine.

### 2. The states – this is the substance of the task
Home is rarely in its full state. Each of these must render honestly:

| State | What Home shows |
|---|---|
| **Guest** (no account) | `GuestHome.dc.html`. Fully usable; sync is not required for anything (hard rule 1) |
| **No car yet** | The Add-car path, not an empty dashboard |
| **Car, zero entries** | No fabricated numbers. No "N/A" tiles – omit a vital rather than print a placeholder |
| **One fill-up, no segment yet (D4)** | ERRORS.md: hint on vitals "One more full tank and your consumption appears", and the card links to capture |
| **Full** | Vitals populated from the engine |

**The "no N/A tiles" rule is a real assertion in the checks** – a tile with nothing honest to show is
omitted, never rendered as "N/A", "–" or "0.0".

### 3. Error/warning states from `docs/ERRORS.md` → Home, copy verbatim
Timeline conflict badge (F9a/S3) with the Trends footnote "1 entry excluded"; possible-duplicate combined
card (S2); archived-car-returned notice (S5); post-outage sync toast (S7); reminder-due banner; the D4
first-estimate hint. **S2/S5/S7 depend on sync, which does not exist until P4** – build the presentation
so the state is reachable and snapshot-testable (a launch argument, as P1.2 and P1.3 did with
`-forceCatalogUnavailable` / `-forceCurrencyLowConfidence`), and say in your report which states are
driven by fixtures rather than real data.

### 4. The odometer formatter – deferred here on purpose
`HANDOVER.md` open item 0: odometer digits are not thousands-grouped. The artboards say `119 486 km`
(thin space); the app renders `119486`. Home displays the same figure as Add car and Edit entry, so
**build one shared formatter and use it everywhere it applies**, rather than a Home-local fix.

Two constraints learned the hard way in P1.3, both non-negotiable:
- **Grouped digits belong in DISPLAY, not in a text field being typed into.** Format on blur, or format
  read-only labels only. A grouped `TextField` is unpleasant to type into.
- **Pin the formatter's locale** (`en_US_POSIX`) exactly as `ManualFillUpFormState` now does, and use a
  **thin space** as the group separator to match the artboard. Do not leave it on `Locale.current`: that
  produced two different decimal separators on one card in P1.3, because typed text is raw while
  formatted text was localised. Presentation-level localisation of numerals is P5's job and must change
  both sides together.
