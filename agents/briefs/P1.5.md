## What to build

### 1. Entry card content – the decided rule
From `docs/DESIGN.md`, verbatim intent:

> Title is the station or vendor; the trailing figure is the amount in DIN. The subtitle line carries
> **quantity · odometer · date**, plus a **paperclip glyph when a receipt or photo is attached**.
> **Fuel kind is shown only when it tells the user something**: when the vehicle accepts more than one
> fuel kind, or when this entry's kind differs from the car's usual.

So `42.0 L · 95 · Aug 23` becomes `42.0 L · 119 486 km · 📎 · Aug 23` for a single-fuel car, and keeps the
fuel kind for a multi-fuel one (petrol + LPG, or a PHEV alternating fuel and electricity).

- The odometer uses **`OdometerFormat.grouped`** – never a locally built formatter.
- The attachment indicator is a **glyph, not a word**, and must carry an accessibility label (colour and
  iconography are never the only channel – `DESIGN.md` accessibility floor).
- An entry with **no odometer** (optional on non-FillUp entries) simply omits that segment. Do not print
  a dash or a zero – same principle as Home's "no N/A tiles".

### 2. Month dividers – currently broken
Today a single "AUGUST" header sits above entries from July and May. Group entries **by calendar month of
`date`**, newest first, one divider each.

Per `docs/DESIGN.md`: **"Monthly dividers carry the month's total spend in DIN."** That total is the sum
of every entry type in that month (fuel, charge, service, expense), in the vehicle's home currency.

**Localisation trap, already paid for once:** P1.4 shipped `"%@ spend"` translated as `"%@ расходы"`,
which rendered "АВГУСТ РАСХОДЫ" – word-order nonsense. A composed month label needs a **full localised
phrase per language**, never concatenation. If you add a divider label, give it a format string whose RU
translation reads naturally, and check it in the RU screenshot.

### 3. Purchase groups
Entries sharing a `purchaseGroupId` are one physical purchase (a mixed receipt: fuel + a car wash).
Render them as **one grouped card** rather than separate rows, so the stream matches the receipt the user
holds.

**Hard rule 4 is the trap here: the fuel amount is NOT the receipt grand total.** The group shows the
grand total; the fuel row inside it shows the fuel amount. Never present the grand total as the fill-up's
cost, and never sum a group into consumption maths. `docs/SCHEMA.md` CHECK 3 is the invariant.

Collapse/expand the group; the L1 checks below cover the collapse state.

### 4. Bottom content inset – a real layout bug
The floating tab bar overlaps the last rows of the stream: the final entry is half-hidden behind it in
both EN and RU (see `design/screenshots/P1.4-home.png`). The scroll content needs a bottom inset so the
last row clears the bar. Verify in the screenshot, not just by reasoning.

keeping the old flag working if any test depends on it).

## Tests

**L1 (`ios/Tests/TankbookCoreTests/`, macOS, no simulator)** – put the grouping/rendering decisions in
`TankbookCore` so they test without a simulator:
- **Ordering**: entries sort by `date` descending, and the union of entry types interleaves correctly.
- **Month grouping**: entries spanning three months produce three dividers, each holding only its own
  month; a month boundary at the 1st and the 31st lands in the right group.
- **Divider totals**: the month total sums every entry type in that month, and a purchase group counts
  its **grand total once**, not once per member row (hard rule 4).
- **Fuel-kind visibility**: hidden for a single-fuel vehicle; shown for a multi-fuel one; shown when the
  entry's kind differs from the vehicle's usual.
- **Odometer segment omitted** when the entry has none.
- **Collapse state**: a collapsed group reports one row, expanded reports its members.

**L4 (`ios/App/UITests/`)**:
- The last row in a long stream is **fully visible and tappable**, not under the tab bar.
- A single-fuel car's rows show no fuel kind; a multi-fuel car's rows do.
- The attachment glyph appears only on entries that have one, and carries its accessibility label.
