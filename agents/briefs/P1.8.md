## What to build

### 1. The duplicate heuristic, exactly as specified
From `docs/SYNC.md` S2, verbatim:

> duplicate heuristic – **same vehicle, dates within 30 min, volume within 5%** – flags the pair.

Implement precisely that. Do not invent extra signals (station, price, odometer) and do not loosen the
bounds. If you believe the heuristic is wrong, say so in your report – **do not change it silently**;
`SYNC.md` is the authority and changing it is a spec decision, not an implementation one.

Put it in `TankbookCore` as a pure function over entries, so every boundary case is L1-testable.

### 2. The combined card
From S2 and `ERRORS.md`:

> the Log shows one combined card: "Possible duplicate – Shell, 42.3 L logged twice" with *Keep both* /
> *Merge* (merge keeps the richer one: **the one with an attachment wins, fields union**).

Render it in the stream where the pair would otherwise appear as two rows. Both actions must be present
and reachable – rule 7: every warning names its next step.

### 3. The invariant that matters most
From S2:

> Until resolved, **only ONE of the pair counts** in consumption and totals, so stats never double.

This is the substance of the task. An unresolved duplicate pair contributes **once** to consumption, to
month totals, and to every derived figure. Choose the counted one deterministically (document which and
why in a comment) so the same data always produces the same numbers.

**"Keep both" means both count from then on** – the user has said they are genuinely two purchases.
**"Merge"** keeps the richer record per the rule above and tombstones the other, so it lands in Recently
deleted (P1.7) and stays recoverable – nothing lost silently.

### 4. Conflict badges (F9a UI)
The amber badge already renders from `TimelineValidator`. Complete it: tapping routes to Edit entry with
the discrepancy visible, and the **"1 entry excluded"** footnote appears wherever excluded entries affect
a figure (Home vitals and, when it exists, Trends). The count must be **real** – derived from the
engine's flags, never hard-coded.

Sync does not exist until P4, so a *duplicate arriving by sync* is fixture-driven; a duplicate created
locally (log the same fill twice) is **real** and must work end to end.

## Tests

**L1 (`ios/Tests/TankbookCoreTests/`, macOS, no simulator)** – the heuristic's boundaries are the point,
so test **both sides** of each:
- 29 min apart flags; **31 min apart does not**.
- Volume within 5% flags; **5.1% apart does not**. Test at a small volume and a large one, since 5% of
  10 L and 5% of 60 L are very different absolute numbers.
- **Different vehicles never flag**, however close the values.
- **The single-count invariant**: two flagged entries contribute **once** to consumption and to the month
  total. Assert the actual figures, not a boolean.
- **Keep both** → both count afterwards. **Merge** → the richer record survives (attachment wins, fields
  union) and the other becomes a tombstone that Recently deleted can restore.
- The counted-one choice is **deterministic**: the same pair in either input order yields the same figures.
- The excluded-count footnote number matches the engine's flag count.

**L4 (`ios/App/UITests/`)**:
- The combined card renders with **both** Keep both and Merge reachable.
- The conflict badge is tappable and routes to Edit entry.
- The "1 entry excluded" footnote shows the real count.

Read the **current** baselines before starting (they rise every task) and report them; both must rise.
`#expect(true)`, or asserting text without asserting the affordance, counts as not done.
