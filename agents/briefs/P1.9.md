## The maths – normative, quoted

From `docs/SCHEMA.md`:

```
SEGMENT    Between consecutive isFull fill-ups (conflict-free, same fuelKind family):
             km     = odo(close) − odo(open)
             liters = Σ volume of every fill after open, up to and including close
             per100 = liters / km × 100
TANK-LEVEL (v1.x refinement) tankLevelAfterPct + tankCapacityL lets a segment close on a
           non-full fill: liters_adjusted = Σ volume + (levelOpen − levelClose)/100 × capacity.
           Gated on tankCapacityL being set; falls back to full-to-full otherwise.
```

Three things that are easy to get wrong and are each a test below:

- **The sign.** `(levelOpen − levelClose)`, in that order. If the tank was fuller at the open than at the
  close, the car burned the difference **in addition to** what was poured in. Reversing this inflates or
  deflates every partial segment silently.
- **`100 ⇒ isFull`.** A level of 100% means the same thing as a full fill; the two representations must
  agree, and a segment closing on a 100% partial must equal the full-to-full result exactly.
- **The gate.** With no `tankCapacityL` there is no litres equivalence and **no adjustment** – fall back
  to full-to-full. Never guess a capacity, and never let a missing capacity produce a wrong number
  instead of no number (hard rule 2's spirit: derive honestly or not at all).

## Tests

**L1 (`ios/Tests/TankbookCoreTests/`, macOS, no simulator)** – this is a maths task, so the L1 suite is
the deliverable:
- **D1–D4 goldens unchanged.** Run them; they must be byte-identical to today.
- **The formula**, worked by hand in the test: a segment opening at 100% and closing at 50% on a 60 L
  tank with 30 L poured → `liters_adjusted = 30 + (100−50)/100 × 60 = 60`. Assert the number.
- **Sign check**: closing *fuller* than it opened yields a **smaller** adjusted litres than the poured
  volume, and the test asserts the direction explicitly.
- **100% partial equals a full fill**: the same history expressed both ways gives the identical headline.
- **No capacity → no adjustment**: the segment falls back to full-to-full, and a partial fill simply does
  not close a segment. Assert the fallback, not just the absence of a crash.
- **Level unset (nil) behaves as before** – this refinement is opt-in per entry.
- A **conflict-flagged** entry stays excluded, as it already is.

**L4 (`ios/App/UITests/`)**:
- The sheet shows the litres equivalence when capacity is known, and **hides it with the hint** when not.
- The percentage the user sets is what gets saved.
