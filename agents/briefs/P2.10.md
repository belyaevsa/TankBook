## The defect, with its fixtures

`receipt-033-lukoil-astana-92-kzt-vat16-kz.jpeg` prints its total in tenge, and the marker `тг`
**OCRs as `гг`**, so `FuelExtraction.currency` stays **nil** on every Kazakh receipt. `receipt-035`
is the same country and card family.

**Why it matters beyond one field:** hard rule 3 - **money is a pair**. A fill-up whose currency was
never detected cannot form one, so the entry either saves in the wrong currency or saves without a
conversion at all. `docs/SCHEMA.md`: `rateSource`/`rate` are meaningless without knowing what the
amount *is*.

## The trap that makes this more than a regex

**A currency guessed from price magnitude is a wrong fact stated confidently.** A tenge total of
`6000.00` and a rouble total of `6000.00` are the same digits; only the document says which. So:

- Detect from **document evidence** - the marker, the VAT rate, the fiscal-QR host (`kofd.kz` is
  Kazakhstan), the language of the surrounding text.
- **Where the document does not say, return nil.** Hard rule 13: a value the app cannot know is
  left for the user, not invented. The corpus already records that a nil beats a confident wrong
  answer (`docs/EXTRACTION.md`, and the `bestTriple` re-baseline that took screenshots 3/3 -> 1/3
  deliberately).
