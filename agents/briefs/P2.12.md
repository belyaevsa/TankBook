## The rule this task exists to serve

`docs/EXTRACTION.md` → "Cross-check: four outcomes, not two", verbatim:

> `liters x unitPrice` against `total`. **The confirm-screen lock has historically treated this as
> a boolean, and that is wrong.** There are four outcomes:

| Outcome | Condition | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **lock** | product == total within tolerance | High confidence. Lock the three numbers together |
| **reconciled** | product - total equals a discount line on the document | Also correct. **Not an error** |
| **mixed** | product < total and the gap is explained by other priced lines | The fuel amount is the fuel line (hard rule 4) |
| **mismatch** | none of the above | Show the residual, name the next step, never block |

> **`reconciled` is not theoretical - it is the majority case on loyalty receipts.** All five
> Circle K app screenshots in the corpus fail a naive cross-check, every one of them by exactly the
> printed discount, while every field was read correctly.

| fixture | line | product | discount | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `screenshot-004` | 1.884 x 67 | 126.23 | 1.01 | 125.22 |
| `screenshot-005` | 2.159 x 58.01 | 125.24 | 4.06 | 121.18 |
| `screenshot-006` | 1.799 x 68 | 122.33 | 1.02 | 121.31 |
| `screenshot-007` | 1.614 x 64 | 103.30 | 1.92 | 101.38 |
| `screenshot-008` | 1.924 x 59.78 | 115.02 | 2.39 (fuel share) | 112.63 |

> **A gate that refuses to lock here is refusing five perfect scans.**

**A sixth instance joined the corpus today** and is worth reading, because it is a *paper* receipt
rather than an app screenshot: `receipts/receipt-038-circlek-sikupilli-95e0-pump8-ee.jpg` prints
`EXTRA SOODUS -0,23 EUR` beneath the item while `K O K K U` still reads **79,32** - the discount is
printed but **not subtracted from the total**. So a parser that reconciles by blindly adding every
discount it finds will get this one wrong in the opposite direction. `reconciled` must mean *the
residual is explained by a discount line*, not *a discount line exists*.

## The two things the cross-check is blind to, and must stay blind to

`Spike/ReceiptSpike/fixtures/README.md`, verbatim - do not "fix" these here:

> The cross-check is a **consistency check, not a correctness one**. It catches a misread digit and
> picks among discrete candidates. It is blind to a **swapped** volume/price pair
> (`a x b == b x a`) and to **lost decimal separators** (scale-invariant).

A swap and a scale shift both satisfy every arithmetic outcome above. Adding `reconciled` must not
be described - in code comments or docs - as making the cross-check more trustworthy about
*correctness*. It makes it stop crying wolf about *consistency*.

## What to build

1. **A four-outcome cross-check result** replacing the boolean, carrying **the residual** and, for
   `reconciled`, **which line explained it**. A bare enum is not enough: the caller needs the
   number to show and the reason.
2. **`reconciled` detection driven by the residual**, not by the presence of a discount line -
   `receipt-038` is the counter-example where a printed discount does *not* explain the total.
3. **`mixed`** per hard rule 4: `product < total`, the gap explained by other priced lines, and the
   fuel amount stays the **fuel line**, never the grand total.
4. **The `screenshot-008` discriminator this batch exposed**: the unit token `L` against Latvian
   `Gab.`, on a fixture whose fuel line is the **third of eight** - so neither "first priced line"
   nor "line nearest the total" finds it. It must return **112.63**, never the grand total 122.99.
5. **Every existing caller of `crossCheckPassed` updated** so no call site quietly keeps the old
   two-outcome reading.

### Vacuous-assertion traps named for this task

- Asserting the outcome enum without asserting the resolved values (named in the task row).
- A `reconciled` implementation that never returns `mismatch` - test 3 exists for that.
- Detecting `reconciled` by "a discount line exists" - `receipt-038` breaks it.
- Loosening the shared tolerance to make a fixture pass. That invalidates the P4.12/P4.13
  measurement and is the one change that quietly breaks three committed comparisons.
- Baselining `high-water.json` from `swift run ReceiptSpike` - the two parsers disagree and the
  ratchet fails instantly. Read the numbers out of `swift test --filter AccuracyRatchet`.
- `#expect(true)`, or asserting only that a call did not throw.
