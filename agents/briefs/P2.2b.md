## The defect

`FuelExtraction.total` and `FuelExtraction.unitPrice` are `Double?`. `docs/SCHEMA.md` types money as
`Decimal` - `FillUp.unitPrice: Decimal?`, `Money.amount: Decimal` - and P2.6's fiscal parser already
does it correctly. **Feeding a `Double` into a `Decimal` field loses precision at the boundary**:
`4201.68` does not survive a binary-float round trip.

**`liters` stays `Double`, and this is not an oversight.** `docs/SCHEMA.md` line 106 types
`volumeL: Double` deliberately - it is a volume, not money. Changing it is out of scope and would be
wrong. **Only `total` and `unitPrice` move.**

Precedent for how to do it: `ios/Sources/TankbookCore/Rates/RemoteRateFetcher.swift` (P5.2a) decodes
wire numbers into `Decimal` exactly, and its test asserts a genuinely lossy value (`1.08107664`)
rather than one that happens to survive `Double`.
