## The rule changed – this is a product-owner correction, dated 2026-08-30

P2.3b built the fuel row as a **limit**: it offers exactly `Vehicle.fuelKinds` and nothing else. The
product owner has corrected that:

> "one car can fillup 95, 92 and 100, actually. so it's not a limit but not a blocker"

**`Vehicle.fuelKinds` is a suggestion, not a constraint** – which is hard rule 13 in its own words:
*the app suggests, the user decides*, and every derived value is a **default input, never a fact**.
A car configured for 95 is routinely filled with 92 or 100; petrol grades share a tank.

What P2.3b got right and you keep:

- Seeds must not describe **impossible cars**. `[.petrol95, .diesel]` is not a car, and P2.3b's
  fix across 11 sites in 7 files stands. That is about how a **car is configured**, which is a
  different question from what a **fill-up may record**.
- A single-kind car renders a static value rather than a chooser, and it stays correctable.
- `fuelGrade` stays a separate field.

## What to build

1. **The offer set becomes "likely", not "allowed".** For a car whose kinds include any petrol
   grade, the row offers **all petrol grades** (92/95/98/100) plus the car's other kinds - so a 95
   car shows 92/95/98/100, and a 95+LPG car shows those four plus LPG. A diesel car offers diesel
   (and its other kinds), not the petrol grades.
2. **Nothing is ever blocked.** Every `FuelKind` stays reachable through the correction affordance,
   on both the multi-kind and the single-kind path. Today the multi-kind chips are a dead end - a
   `[95, LPG]` car cannot record anything else - and that is the defect this task exists to fix.
   The two paths must end up **equally permissive**; P2.3b left them inverted, with the more
   constrained car getting the more permissive editor.
3. **Remove the hard refusal in the fuel pills.** P2.3b renders the conflicting pill `.disabled`,
   so diesel + petrol is refused outright. A refusal is a blocker, and the correction above says
   there are none. **Assumption to implement, flagged to the owner:** diesel + petrol stays
   *discouraged but savable*. If you can express the discouragement without blocking (the pill
   selectable, with the combination clearly not the normal case), do that; if the only honest
   options are "block" or "silent", choose **silent** and say so in your report - a wrong car
   configuration is correctable and is not a data-integrity failure.
4. **Rewrite the `docs/SCHEMA.md` paragraph P2.3b added** (search for "The input rule (P2.3b").
   It currently states the limit as the rule and says diesel+petrol "is rejected". It must state
   the corrected rule, carry the owner's reason (petrol grades share a tank; a 95 car takes 92 and
   100), and keep the surrounding "offer set, not the car's fuel" paragraph coherent - that section
   is the authority and a stale sentence in it is more expensive than a bug.
