## What to build

### 1. Detection
Decide whether a receipt is mixed. Two independent signals, and they must combine:

- **The QR grand total exceeds the fuel line** (`.suggestsMixedReceipt`). Strong, exact, but only
  when a QR is present - **9 of 22 corpus receipts have one**, so it cannot be the only path.
- **Line-item structure**: a non-fuel product line alongside the fuel line. This is the path that
  works without a QR, and it is where `receipt-009`'s 129.00 collision bites.

Detection must be **conservative**: a false "this is mixed" splits a plain fill-up into two
entries and corrupts the user's log. When unsure, treat it as a normal receipt and let the user
say otherwise - hard rule 13.

### 2. "Also on this receipt" UI
Per the artboard, inside the existing Confirm sheet: the fuel line stays the fill-up, and the
remaining lines are offered as **separate Expenses the user can accept or dismiss individually**.
Nothing is created without the user's action. Rule 15's sibling principle applies: the sheet must
still be an ordinary Confirm sheet when nothing is detected.

### 3. Grouped save
On save, the `FillUp` and any accepted `Expense`s **share a `purchaseGroupId`** and the
attachment. Two invariants, both L1-testable and both required:

- **The fill-up's amount is the FUEL LINE, never the grand total** (hard rule 4).
- **The sum of the group's amounts is <= the receipt's grand total.** Never more.

Non-car lines the user dismisses are simply not logged; the full receipt stays readable in the
attachment.
