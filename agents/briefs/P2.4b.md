## Why this task exists – the corpus proved it, in `fixtures/receipts/README.md` § 5b

`receipt-015` and `receipt-026` are **two genuine, separate fills** - same chain, same day, same
fuel, **same volume and same total to the kopeck** - at stations ~100 km apart:

| | receipt-026 | receipt-015 |
|---|---|---|
| time | 11.07.26 **10:29** | 11.07.26 **11:03** |
| line | `269.00*20` | `269.00*20` |
| total | 5380.00 | 5380.00 |
| ФД / ФП | 79802 / 1119386949 | 30045 / 4064907347 |

`DuplicateDetector.isDuplicate` keys on *same vehicle · within 30 minutes · volume within 5%*.
These are **34 minutes apart with identical volume**, so they escape a false merge offer **by four
minutes**. Move either fill slightly and the app offers to merge two real purchases - and a
merge is a data loss the user has to notice to undo.

**Two different fiscal identities are a PROOF the purchases differ, not a heuristic.** That is the
whole point: `fn`+`i`+`fp` identify a fiscal document, and two documents cannot be one purchase.

## What to build

`isDuplicate` gains one rule, **ahead of the heuristic**:

- **Both fills carry an identity and the identities differ -> NOT duplicates.** Full stop, no
  matter how identical the amounts, times and volumes are. A proof outranks a heuristic.
- **Both carry an identity and they are equal -> the same document.** These are duplicates
  regardless of the 30-minute window (the same receipt scanned twice, minutes or days apart).
- **Either identity is nil -> the existing heuristic decides, byte-for-byte unchanged.** This is
  the common path and it must not shift at all.

### Vacuous-assertion traps for this task

- `#expect(true)`, or asserting only that a call did not throw.
- Testing only the **veto** direction (different identities are not duplicates) and never the
  **equality** direction. A rule that always returns "not a duplicate" would pass a one-sided
  suite and silently switch duplicate detection off.
- Using made-up fiscal ids when the real ones are printed in § 5b above. Use the real ones and
  cite the fixture names in the test.
- Asserting the timestamps are 34 minutes apart without also testing the **four-minute** case -
  34 minutes passes today, and a test that passes before your change proves nothing.
- Loosening or "improving" the 30-minute / 5% heuristic. It is out of scope; a nil-identity pair
  must behave exactly as it does today.
