## Two bugs, both diagnosed. You are implementing, not investigating.

### Bug 1 – the database is reset MORE THAN ONCE per launch, so seeds wipe each other

`-homeResetDatabase` is passed on every capture launch. **Five** types call
`AppStore.resetForTests()`:

```
ios/App/Sources/Home/HomeTestSeed.swift
ios/App/Sources/RecentlyDeleted/RecentlyDeletedTestSeed.swift
ios/App/Sources/TankLevel/TankLevelView.swift
ios/App/Sources/TireSets/TireSetTestSeed.swift        (P3.3, merged today)
ios/App/Sources/Reminders/ReminderTestSeed.swift      (P3.4, merged today)
```

Only three carry a `didResetForLaunch` flag, and **each flag is private to its own type**. So a
launch can reset the database several times: seed A resets and seeds, then seed B resets again and
wipes A's data. The screen renders empty, and the capture still reports `ok`.

This is not theoretical. `P1.4-home.png`, captured today with `-seedHomeFullHistory`, rendered the
**empty** Home state. The bug appeared the moment two more resetting seeds merged.

**The fix: one reset gate for the whole process.** Put it on `AppStore` - a single
`resetForTestsOncePerLaunch()` that performs the reset at most once, and have all five call sites
use it. Delete the three per-type flags; they are the bug. A seed's own "already seeded, bail"
check stays as it is - that part is correct and idempotent.

### Bug 2 – the EN captures render in Russian

`scripts/capture-screenshots.sh`'s `capture()` passes `-AppleLanguages "(ru)" -AppleLocale ru_RU`
for a `ru` row and **passes nothing at all** for an `en` row. The RU setting therefore persists,
and every `en` capture that follows a `ru` one renders in Russian. Today that includes
`P1.4-home.png` and `P3.2-parts-shelf.png`.

**The fix:** the `en` branch passes `-AppleLanguages "(en)" -AppleLocale en_US` explicitly. Symmetry,
not cleverness - never rely on the absence of an argument to undo a previous argument.
