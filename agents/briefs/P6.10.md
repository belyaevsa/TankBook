## Five non-negotiables, each a way this ships wrong

1. **EN and RU through the String Catalog** (hard rule 10). Russian runs 20-30% longer and this is a
   multi-line notice - it is the shape that overflows. Check Dynamic Type **XL** too (P6.13).
2. **Never amber.** Amber is attention/warning only (`docs/DESIGN.md`); "this is alpha" is a
   **disclosure, not a fault**. Use neutral/`inkSoft` treatment.
3. **It must not frame capture as the failure branch, nor discourage it.** Scanning and typing are
   **peers** (hard rule 15) and the whole point is to keep captures coming. "Scanning often fails,
   type instead" is the wrong notice.
4. **Never mid-capture and never on the Confirm sheet.** A notice between the shutter and the
   result is where it becomes a nag, and `docs/ERRORS.md` already forbids interrupting there. It
   belongs on the **capture surface**, before the shutter.
5. **It is not an error**, so no next-step action bar and no amber. But **decide the "send us this
   case" path explicitly** rather than by omission: the feedback endpoint exists
   (`docs/API.md` → feedback), and the product owner asked for cases. Either wire it or state why
   not.

**Placement, persistence and dismissal are open** - decide them, and record the decision in
`docs/ERRORS.md` and `docs/SCREENMAP.md` in the same change. A notice that cannot be dismissed and
never goes away is a nag; one that appears once and is never seen again teaches nothing. Say what
you chose and why.

## The blind spot that has now shipped SIX times

Read the top of `ios/App/Sources/Localization/L10n.swift` before writing a string.
**`Text(_: String)` does not localise.** A string *literal* is a `LocalizedStringKey` and localises;
an **interpolated `String`** does not, and the gate cannot see the difference because no key is
missing. The two most recent instances were both in the import feature this week -
`Text("\(odometer) km")` rendering Latin `km` in Russian, twice, in two different files.
