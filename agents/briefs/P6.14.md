## The gap, found by P2.11 rather than by reading the code

`CorpusABScorer.score` scores **only** `liters`, `unitPrice` and `total`. But `FuelExtraction` also
carries **`fuelKind`** and **`currency`**, and both are real extracted fields the product depends
on:

- **`fuelKind`** drives the parser vocabulary, the fuel chips on Confirm, and whether the kind is
  printed on every log row (`docs/SCHEMA.md` → Vehicle catalog, the offer-set paragraph).
- **`currency`** is half of hard rule 3 - money is a pair, and a fill-up with no detected currency
  cannot form one.

**Neither can move the number that guards extraction.** P2.11 fixed fuel-kind resolution on
`receipt-034` and the receipts score stayed flat at 48/106 - correctly, and that is the defect. The
same will happen to **P2.10 (KZT currency detection)** when it lands, which is why this task goes
first: the gate should be able to see the fix before the fix is written.

## The trap this task is most likely to fall into

**Adding scored fields without re-baselining reads as a sudden accuracy collapse.** Receipts go from
48/106 to 48/(106 + however many new fields) the instant you add columns, and every one of those new
fields is a miss until the extractor is improved. That is expected and correct - it is not a
regression, and it must not be "fixed" by:

- leaving the new columns empty everywhere (a scored field nobody asserts is not scored at all);
- loosening `AccuracyRatchet.violation`;
- touching the tolerance;
- or improving the extractor to make the number look better (a different task).

**Write the honest number and explain it in the `_note`.**

### Vacuous-assertion traps

- New columns left empty across the board (see above).
- Asserting the hit direction without the miss direction (test 1).
- Changing the shared tolerance to make anything pass.
- Editing `Extraction/` to raise the score.
- `#expect(true)`, or asserting only that a call did not throw.
