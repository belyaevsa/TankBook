## The two defects

Both are in the anomaly dismiss sheet. **EN is correct and must not change.**

**(a) `Changed tyres` → «Поменял шины» is masculine past tense.** A woman dismissing the card reads
a form that misgenders her. It is also the odd one out: the other three options are nouns
(`Зима`, `Буксировка`, `Прочее`). Russian has **no genderless past tense**, so a verb here cannot
be repaired by translation - the sentence shape is wrong. Making it a noun phrase - **«Замена
шин»** - fixes the gender and the inconsistency in one edit.

**(b) `Dismissing with a reason keeps it quiet for this period.` →
«Отклонение с причиной скроет подсказку на этот период.»** **«Отклонение» means *deviation*** in
exactly this domain, and the sentence sits directly beneath a card about a consumption
**deviation** - so it reads as "a deviation with a cause" rather than "dismissing with a reason".
Proposed: **«Если укажете причину, подсказка скроется на этот период.»**

This is the same class as P4.7's «с вашего телефон Android»: **the grammar is legal and the meaning
is wrong.** Only reading the rendered screen finds it.

You may improve on the two proposed strings if you can justify it, but you must keep: no gendered
verb form for any option, and no word that collides with the domain vocabulary.

«отклонени» in any form. That is a guard against the class, which is what this repo asks for -
