## What to build

1. **The card, in the Log** - never a push, never a modal, never mid-capture. It states the drift in
   the user's own units and names the window it compared (the engine gives you both windows; say
   which, or the number is unfalsifiable to the reader).
2. **Evidence on tap** - the drift made visible. The engine hands you `rollingValue`,
   `baselineValue` and both windows; you do not compute anything.
3. **Two next steps, both real** (hard rule 7): **act** creates a service reminder, and **dismiss
   with a reason** records an `AnomalyDismissal`. A card with only "dismiss" teaches nothing; a card
   with only "act" is a nag.
4. **Dismissal reasons the user would actually pick** - "it's winter", "changed tyres", "towing" -
   plus a free-text path. `ReminderLifecycle.dismiss(reason:)` is the existing precedent for
   carrying a reason; follow it rather than inventing a second shape.
5. **Persist the dismissal, never the verdict** (hard rule 2 - stats are derived). The engine
   recomputes; your job is to remember what the user said, not what the engine concluded.

### Vacuous traps

- Asserting the card exists without asserting its **text** - the classic way a localization bug
  stays green.
- Testing only the anomaly case. Test 1 is the common path and the one that proves you did not make
  the card render unconditionally.
- Persisting the verdict instead of the dismissal (hard rule 2); it goes stale the moment an entry
  is edited.
- Recomputing the drift in the view instead of using the engine's numbers - two implementations of
  one calculation is how they diverge.
- `#expect(true)`.

## Two blind spots this codebase has actually shipped

- **`Text(_: String)` does not localise.** A *literal* is a `LocalizedStringKey`; an **interpolated
  `String`** is not, and the localization gate cannot tell them apart because no key is missing.
  **Six instances have shipped**, two of them this week - both `Text("\(number) km")`. A card
  full of formatted numbers is exactly this shape. Read the top of `L10n.swift`.
- **`swift test --filter <UITestName>` matches zero tests and exits 0.** XCUITests are invisible to
  the package runner; use `xcodebuild -only-testing:`. A filter that matches nothing reads as a pass.
