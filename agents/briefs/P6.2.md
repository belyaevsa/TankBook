## The traps this feature has, named

- **A month with no entries must produce NO notification**, not a summary saying zero. A push that
  tells the user nothing happened is a nag.
- **Multiple vehicles**: decide deliberately - one notification or one per car - and write the
  decision into `NOTIFICATIONS.md`. Do not leave it implied by the code.
- **Money**: hard rule 3 says `rateDate` is the entry date, never "today". A monthly total must not
  re-convert at today's rate.
- **Permission**: `NOTIFICATIONS.md` governs when permission is asked. Do not ask at launch and do
  not ask on toggling if that contradicts the doc - read it.
- **Turning the toggle OFF must cancel the pending request**, not merely stop scheduling new ones.
