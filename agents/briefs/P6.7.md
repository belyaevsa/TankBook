## Part 1 (P6.7) - cyan means electric

`DESIGN.md` says `headlight` encodes **electric**, and that a fuel card never shows cyan. But ~66 of
its uses across ~25 files in `ios/App/Sources` are buttons, links and inert icons; **only two are
genuinely electric** (the `powertrain == .ev` ternaries). The root cause is that the palette has no
interactive colour - both accents are reserved by DESIGN.md's own rules, so there was nothing else
to reach for.

1. Add an **`action`** colour to `design/tokens.json`: dark **`#8FB4D9`**, light **`#2F6690`**.
   Already verified against every ground: dark 8.60:1 on midnight and 7.64:1 on dash; light 5.67:1
   and 6.13:1. Use these values.
2. Re-point every **non-electric** `headlight` use to `action`, or to `inkSoft` where the element is
   inert rather than interactive. Judge each site; this is not a blind find-and-replace.
3. Leave the genuinely electric uses on `headlight`. Those are the point of the token.
4. Write the rule into `docs/DESIGN.md` in the same change - a decision that lives only in a diff
   gets reverted by the next person who needs a blue.

every future addition to it is a deliberate act. Also assert both tokens resolve to the exact hexes
in both themes.
