## The problem, measured

The EN landing page ships **1601 KB** of screenshots, served raw:

```
477 KB  P2.1-capture.png      332 KB  P1.4-home.png       290 KB  P2.5-confirm-foreign.png
271 KB  P2.3-confirm.png      228 KB  P1.3-confirm-manual.png
```

`site/layouts/_partials/screenshot.html` calls `.RelPermalink` on the source resource, so Hugo serves
the originals untouched. **Format is the smaller half of the waste**: these are 1206x2622 phone
captures rendering into slots roughly 300-340 px wide. Resizing matters more than converting, and
doing both is the task.

`docs/SITE.md` already specifies **WebP with a PNG fallback**. Hugo cannot encode AVIF - do not try.

## What to build

Rework `screenshot.html` so every screenshot is:

1. **Resized** to the width it actually renders at, with a `srcset` carrying 1x and 2x. Do not ship
   1206 px into a 340 px slot.
2. **Served as WebP with a PNG fallback**, via `<picture>` + `<source type="image/webp">`.
3. Still carrying its existing guards - the partial must keep calling `errorf` on a missing
   resource, an empty `alt`, or `alt` equal to the literal word "screenshot". **Those guards are
   mutation-tested and must keep failing the build.**
4. Still emitting explicit `width`/`height` so layout does not shift while images load.

The partial is the **only** path to an `<img>` on this site and must stay that way - a check asserts
`<img` appears in no other file.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **Do not convert the OG images.** Social crawlers - Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Telegram - have patchy
  WebP support, and an OG card some platforms cannot render is worse than a heavy one they can.
  Leave `og:image` as PNG.
- Do not touch the generated ambience under `generated/` - already processed to WebP.
- Do not change any copy, any layout, or any content file.

## Tighten the gate you are about to make passable

`scripts/check-site.sh` has a `ASSET_CAP_KB=800` cap. Once the screenshots shrink, 800 KB is a cap
nothing can hit, which is a gate that has stopped asking a question. **Lower it to a value that would
catch a regression** - pick it from the measured result, not from a round number - and
**mutation-test your own cap**: set it below the largest shipped asset, confirm `check-site.sh`
exits non-zero, restore. A cap that cannot fail is worse than none, and this exact gate was already
found vacuous once on this project.
