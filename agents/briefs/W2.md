## What to build

**S3 - the legal pages, EN and RU** (`privacy`, `terms`, `support`, `delete-account`, plus
`roadmap` and `press` bodies). Hand-written Russian, never machine-translated.

The privacy page must state, in both languages, each of these - and **each is true of this app
today**, so do not soften or embellish:

- The app is fully usable **with no account**; the local database is authoritative.
- Sign-in is optional and enables sync, restore and the cloud extraction gateway. We store the
  account id, email, and the synced record stream, TLS + encrypted at rest. **No end-to-end
  encryption in v1** - say so plainly rather than implying more.
- **We never log domain values**: amounts, stations, notes, coordinates, payloads, tokens, images -
  at any level, in any build.
- Images sent to the extraction gateway are **processed transiently and never retained**.
- **The one exception**: third-party import parsing. An uploaded file and its parse result **are
  stored, deliberately, for 30 days**, matching the tombstone and undo window. No sign-in required.
- Deleting an account is a **tombstone**: other devices learn via `410`, and the user's log **stays
  on their phone**.
- Export is always free, CSV and JSON. No ads. No server-side analytics.
- **Analytics on this website: state that the site sets no cookies and runs no third-party
  analytics.** That is currently true and must stay true; the decision to add any is not yours.

`/terms/`: plain language, and **no subscription clauses** - the Pro tier is deferred and selling
something that does not exist is worse than saying nothing.

`/support/`: the address is **`to@belyaev.live`**, and it is also the App Store listing's support

`/roadmap/`: shipped / next / **deliberately cut**. Pump-display capture belongs under "next"
