## You have an image generation tool. Use it, within one hard boundary.

**Generated pixels are BASE LAYERS ONLY. Everything that carries meaning is composited on top.**
`docs/SITE.md` -> "Imagery: what may be generated, and what may never be" is the authority. In short:

**Generate:** atmospheric ground with no claim in it - a night forecourt's light on wet tarmac, a dark
road gradient, grain and texture fields, the ambient wash behind a section, the canvas an OG card sits on.

**Never generate:**

- **Product UI of any kind.** A generated "app screen" is a fabricated mockup - forbidden - and it
  asserts the product looks like something it does not. Real captures live in `design/screenshots/`.
- **Text. Any text.** No headlines, no wordmark, no UI labels, no receipt contents. Models garble
  glyphs and garble **Cyrillic** far worse than Latin, so the Russian page would carry damage an
  English reader never notices. Text is live HTML, always.
- **People as users, testimonials, star ratings, press logos, App Store badges.** There is no App
  Store listing and no users yet. Generating social proof is inventing it.
- **Watermarks.** Generate without them. If one appears, **discard and regenerate** - never crop.
  A cropped watermark is still someone else's mark.

## Palette: constrain, then verify

Name the Night Drive values in every prompt - `midnight #101318`, `dash #1A1F27`,
`taillight #F4503A`, `headlight #4FC3E8`, `ink #EAEDF2`. Exact values are in `design/tokens.json`.

**A prompt is a request, not a guarantee.** After generating, sample the image's dominant colours and
compare against those tokens. An off-palette image is **regenerated, not colour-graded** - grading a
wrong image reliably produces a muddy right one. Report the sampled values.
