# PU.91 review – row locator diagnosis and next round

## 1. Verdict on the problem statement

**Partly right.** The dark heads have a real location and display-decision problem, but the current *model-ranking* bottleneck is the interaction between proposed quads, the verifier and slicer, role assignment, and the law. Rotated IoU and digit containment are inadequate proxies for that interaction. The law is doing valuable safety work; a `nothingClosed` or `priceUnvalidated` ledger label says what rejected the *result*, not which upstream stage caused it. Keep the app-path heldout score and the per-photo ledger as the shipping criteria, with the simulator as the measured runtime. Add intermediate traces to diagnose causality. Do not relax closure merely to recover cells.

Evidence:

* On hand quads, modest enlargement preserves 159–160 correct committed cells while small cuts and shifts lose 6–36 cells (brief, observation 1). This establishes an asymmetric *oracle-box* response. It does not establish that enlarging *predicted* boxes works: `r1-margin-h0.3-v0.1.json` commits 123/121 versus `r1.json` 126/125, and the still wider `h0.5-v0.15` commits 104/103. In `PumpReader.swift`, `sliceDetectedOrOriginal` accepts a wider strip whenever its occupied-cell count is at least the plain strip's count; equal count says nothing about whether those are the right glyphs. The verifier also sees the changed strip. This explains why a box can contain more digits and produce a worse read without blaming closure itself.
* `segeval.py` chooses pixel/link thresholds on validation stills for recall@0.7 under a false-row ceiling; `rotgate.py` scores overlap with hand quads. Neither metric sees the widened 96 px strip, kept/rejected verifier rows, assigned roles, or the law. D improves r1's median IoU, recall, false rows and coverage, yet `D-F.json` is 134/132 versus `r1-F.json` 140/138. B's 195/252 coverage versus r1's 160/252 accompanies `B-F.json` 115/112, including 28 right-to-abstained “law flips” and two assignment flips in the r1-F diff. Thus “maximize containment” is also the wrong sole objective.
* The original r1-to-r2 ledger (`runs/2026-09-29/pu91-r2-live-diff.txt`) has 14 changed photos. r2 gains complete reads on `pump-089`, `104`, `120`, `170` and a pair on `056`; it loses complete reads on `068`, `095`, `165`, `201`, and introduces wrong `pump-055` litres and total. The losses end in law refusals or `notADisplay`, but the ledger does **not** preserve the intermediate strips or row values. Similar row IoU and no extra rows on the ten named disagreement stills rule out gross missing-row explanations there, not pixel-level read changes or changed verifier/roles. In the F comparison, `r1-F` → `r2-F` changes only eight stills and finishes 140/138 → 139/136: r2 gains `056` and `104`, loses `095` and `175`, and changes `055` total from correct to wrong. These are different effects from a four-cell scalar difference.
* The dark-family problem remains: PU.90 records missed black-LCD rows and advert rows on TFT (`agents/research/PU.90.md` §1); PU.91's `docs/TASKS.md` row reports dark heldout2 recall 15/27 → 19/27 and TFT `pump-354` 0/3 → 3/3. A 68-photo general heldout is not enough evidence of field improvement on these rare families. The dark record disagreement file contains 2,078 frames from 30 records, but many are adjacent frames of one head, not 2,078 independent examples; `video-050` is 166/313 agreement, `video-052` 425/520, and two records have zero agreement. Agreement with a drifting tracker is a proposal filter, not ground truth.
* F is a credible path-level gain, not a locator improvement. `r1.json` → `r1-F.json` is 126/125 → 140/138 on macOS: 13 newly right cells and one newly wrong, all in five photos (`pump-055`, `089`, `120`, `170`, `175`). The wrong `055` litres is cautioned. The simulator result in the brief is 128/127 → 142/140, so the runtime to promote on is the simulator. `PumpDisplayCapture.classify` reruns the full decision/read below three committed fields and chooses the read with a larger committed count, retaining the first on a tie. That rule is effective here, but has no intrinsic safeguard against a three-field incorrect read winning over a two-field correct one. It doubled the reported live-run time from 228 to 438 seconds.

**Uncertainty:** the 183 scored cells are clustered within 68 photos; triples from one photo are strongly dependent. For F the effective gain is four newly successful photos plus one mixed photo, not 13 independent trials. D-F's six-cell deficit against r1-F comes from losing `pump-050`, `120`, `165` triples while gaining `104`; B-F's 25-cell deficit spans many more distinct photos and is a stronger rejection. The small r1-F versus r2-F and D-F gaps can plausibly sit within seed and sample variability. F's paired, zero-loss right-cell pattern is more promising, but five affected photos and one new wrong do not prove population benefit. C1/C2 must be compared to r1 on the *same* baseline and to each other, ideally both with F, by paired still outcomes and per-head buckets. If their spread or churn resembles r2/D's 1–6-cell gaps, those model ranks are unresolved. If both controls reproduce r1 closely while r2 consistently loses the same ordinary photos, the data change is implicated. If controls themselves move whole triples, training instability is the first problem. No C1/C2 scored ledger is present in `runs/2026-09-30/ledgers/` at review time.

## 2. Ranked next experiments

All candidates should report a simulator app-path ledger diff against shipped r1 and r1+F, 183 scored cells, committed/right, precision at least 0.98, zero new uncautioned wrong cells, per-photo gains/losses, dark/ordinary buckets, non-pump false displays, and phone latency. Run diagnostic comparisons on train-side validation or a separately frozen development set; use the 68 heldout and heldout2 as confirmation, not repeated hyperparameter search. A new independent set of dark *fills* from different heads is necessary to claim family improvement.

End-to-end differentiable training against final law success is premature: the law is discrete, the heldout has only 68 independent stills, and this would overfit a few whole-photo closures. A reader-guided *offline reranker* or boundary loss trained on train-side strips is the smaller falsifiable step. Multi-seed ensembling and locator test-time augmentation likewise deserve a trial only after the paired traces show complementary correct proposals; averaging masks can merge neighbouring board rows, and extra proposals change role assignment and false-display rate. A separate dark model/head requires a reliable routing decision and several independent dark heads; test after the data round. TFT should follow PU.92's family-specific glyph route, since PU.90 records proportional TFT text and advert interference rather than a generic RowSeg-only defect.

## 3. Corpus growth

**Grow it, but in independent heads and labelled failure modes, not thousands of near-duplicate tracked frames.** The current r1→r2 difference adds only 28 train stills, 13 verified frames and 12 negatives (`models.json`, PU.91 row), despite the dark problem's diversity. Prioritize roughly 30–50 new dark *fill* stills across at least 8–12 heads, brands, stations, exposure/glare conditions and viewing angles, with total/litres/price and hand quads. Reserve whole heads/records, not frames, for an untouched dark test group. Seek several TFT makes separately for PU.92; one Terminal design does not validate a TFT route. These are planning targets for an interpretable next learning curve, not a claimed power calculation.

Next, verify a modest stratified sample of frames from those new records, about 10–30 per record chosen for actual appearance changes, glare, motion and disagreement. Existing verified frames are useful, but adjacent frames share the same head and cannot replace new stills. Use `seg-r2-disagreements.json` to queue owner review, particularly `video-050`, `052`, `053` and zero-agreement records, while sampling some agreement frames too so the audit can estimate false agreement. Do not use unverified tracked boxes at scale as truth: `segdata.py` explicitly treats `--pseudo` as experimental and documents tracker drift, and agreement of two predictors does not remove correlated bias. E's 266 pseudo frames are a useful **ablation**, scored against a no-pseudo arm at equal seed and steps; if dark holdout recall rises without independent-head app-path gains, it has failed. Audit a random subset of pseudo labels for boundary and wrong-row errors before any expansion.

Add targeted non-pump negatives only when traces show false rows or false display verdicts on receipts, adverts, boards and stickers; more generic negatives alone do not repair dark-row misses. Synthetic dark styles can cheaply probe whether colour/contrast is the issue, but validate on real unseen heads before including them in a shipping training mix. Plot a learning curve at 0, 25, 50 and 100 new independent dark stills with fixed controls/seeds, reporting dark read success, ordinary-photo paired losses, and false rows. Stop collecting the same visual mode when the curve plateaus; collect the next failure mode instead. More data cannot be said to “move the number” until that independent-head curve and app ledger move together.

## 4. Dead ends

* Shipping r2, D or B because their overlap or containment rises. r2 falls below the 0.98 macOS precision floor without F (127/130), and D/B lose app-path reads with F. The simulator remains decisive for any future candidate.
* Global read-margin widening or padded targets as presently implemented. Four A settings lose 3–22 correct cells versus r1; B loses 26 correct cells versus r1-F despite best digit coverage. A narrow, law-gated retry is a different experiment and F supports it.
* Relaxing arithmetic closure, price bands or the display verdict to recover abstentions. The ledgers provide no evidence those guards are wrong on the lost photos, while PU.91's `pump-055` illustrates the cost of a plausible wrong read. Trace upstream values first.
* Bulk pseudo-labeling of thousands of correlated tracked frames, or treating per-frame agreement as independent validation. It risks teaching tracker drift and would inflate apparent sample size.

## 5. Measurement and code corrections

* The brief's “flips are not location failures” is too categorical. Similar IoU on ten photos excludes large matching failures; a few pixels can still change glyph pitch, verifier geometry or a threshold crossing. `pump-201` is explicitly `right → notADisplay` in the original diff. The proper diagnosis requires raw row/strip traces, not reason labels alone.
* The brief calls losses “in the law and display decision, not as misread digits.” The ledger has *final refusal reasons*, not raw digit strings. `pump-live-diff.py` classifies a right-to-abstained field using the after-reason; it cannot distinguish a law defect from bad upstream inputs. `PumpReadingLaw.swift`'s pair tier deliberately refuses out-of-band and unvalidated prices, so loosening it from this evidence would be unsafe.
* The ledger denominator is **183 scored cells**, with 21 unscored fields in each 68-photo file, not 204 independent opportunities. `PumpReaderPipelineTests+Live.swift` excludes `csvDisagrees` and scores cautioned wrong cells as wrong; it uses an unlimited verifier budget for deterministic Debug tests, whereas the production slow path has a 1.5 s cap (`PumpDisplayCapture.swift`). Check phone latency and deadline failures before calling a simulator ledger fully representative of capture time.
* F's second pass reruns `decide` and verifier, since margins affect `reader.verify`; it is not simply a second CRNN call. `classify` returns the *first* `Detection` even when choosing the second `Reading` (`PumpDisplayCapture.swift`), so inspect app consumers and traces for an inconsistent display flag or crop provenance. The current live harness scores `.reading` and treats nil as `notADisplay`; it does not establish consistency of the returned detection pair. This is a concrete review seam, not evidence of a current counted error.
* “No wrong cell without a caution” and precision are distinct checks. F's 138/140 macOS precision is 0.986, while r2's 127/130 is 0.977; the simulation figures in the brief differ by a few cells. Report committed, correct, wrong, cautioned wrong, uncautioned wrong and per-photo changes together, and do not compare the 186-point ship score with the 183-cell live numerator as if they had the same denominator.
