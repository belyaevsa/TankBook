## Sequence trace

Fresh iPhone → Welcome "Already use Tankbook? Restore your garage" (`WelcomeRootView.swift:39`) → `SignInSheet(arrivedViaRestore: true)` → Sign in with Apple → session saved (`SignInFlow.swift:240-242`) → no local data → `runRestore` (`:255`) → `SyncRestoreProvider.restore` pulls from cursor 0 in 500-record pages, attachments arriving as inline-thumbnail records only, zero blob fetches → **while this runs the user stares at the Apple button's spinner** (phase `.signingIn`, `SignInFlow.swift:237`) → pull returns `.restored(stats)` → `RestoringView` "Welcome back / Found in your account: cars, entries, last odometer" (no source device, photo bar at 0%) → "Open my garage" → dismiss → the restored garage renders from the local DB. Old phone, in parallel: Settings → Account & devices → Revoke → the old phone's next pull is 410 and it signs out locally, keeping its local log.

**Where a fact stops being carried:**
1. **The source device.** The journey promises "last odometer with its source device - from your Android phone, yesterday". `RestoreStats.compute` produces `lastOdometerKm` and `lastOdometerDaysAgo`, but the device name is never computed (v2), and `sourceSuffix` gates on the nil name so the whole suffix - recency included - drops to `""`. The screen renders "Last odometer N km" and nothing else.
2. **The export fallback.** The user who exported their data holds a Tankbook archive, but the "import a file you exported yourself" next step hands them to `ImportWizardView`, which asks "which app is this from?" and parses MFM/Drivvo. The archive (a `manifest.json` + `data.json` + `attachments/` directory, `SCHEMA.md:812-819`) and the Tankbook CSVs are neither pickable (`fileImporter` types) nor parseable by that server. The fallback chain ends at the export; there is no door that reads it back.
