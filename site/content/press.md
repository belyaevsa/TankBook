## The name

**Tankbook** – one word, capital T. The site is tankbook.live.

## One-paragraph description

Tankbook is a car cost log for iPhone that keeps your data on your phone and works with no account.
Fill-ups, charging, service and the rest are added in seconds through two equal doors – snap the
receipt, or type it in – and every entry is checked by arithmetic you can watch: litres × price has
to equal the total. It reads petrol, diesel, hybrid and electric cars in one history, keeps both
amounts when you fill up abroad, exports everything in CSV or JSON for free, and never asks you to
sign in first.

## Facts

- **Platform:** iPhone, iOS 18 or later.
- **Price:** free; nothing is for sale today.
- **Account:** not required. Optional sign-in enables sync and restore.
- **Languages:** English and Russian, both from day one.
- **Status:** not on the App Store yet – TestFlight is opening soon.
