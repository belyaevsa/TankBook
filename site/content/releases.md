## 1.1 – in preparation

The next update. It is being prepared for the App Store now; this is what it carries.

### New

- **Photograph the pump display.** No receipt? Point the camera at the pump: the total, litres and
  price it can read are pre-filled for you to check against the photo before saving. The reading
  happens on your phone. It is a head start, not an answer – typing the numbers stays one tap away.
- **A warning when the numbers do not add up.** If a pump reading's litres × price does not reach its
  total, the check screen says so, and a number the app could not read is left for you to type.
- **Service invoices from Photos.** An invoice you already have as a photo, a screenshot or a PDF
  page opens as a service entry with its lines split out for you to review – and pages can be added.
- **Reminders on the Log.** Every due reminder shows with its date or distance, one tap from the
  full list.
- **The month at a glance.** Distance, consumption, cost per kilometre and the comparison with the
  previous month – shown only when your records are enough to support it.
- **Fuel prices by station brand** in Trends, once you have entries from two brands.
- **Appearance:** match the system, or always dark, or always light.

### Better

- **Check scanned numbers beside the photo** before the entry opens: zoom, turn, correct.
- **An invoice shot in Fill-up mode** is recognised as not being a fuel receipt, and you can open it
  as a service or an expense instead of an empty fuel entry.
- **Share a CSV from another app** straight into the import.
- **Finish a reminder by scanning** its invoice or receipt, with the reminder's details carried in.
- **Restores are more patient:** recent photos are ready after a restore or sync, and low-power and
  low-data settings are respected.

### Fixed

- A service photo chosen from Photos opens a service entry, not a fill-up.
- An invoice's VAT is no longer taken for its total, and its table rows and issuer are read more
  carefully.
- A pump reading that cannot be trusted is never silently filled in from other text on the photo.
- Consumption uses the unit you chose for the car – MPG, km/L or L/100 km – everywhere.
- A restore to a new phone no longer stalls when an entry arrives before its car, and an interrupted
  restore says what arrived and offers Retry.
- A sync the server rejects is shown, not retried forever in silence.

## 1.0 – 22 September 2026

The first release.

- **Your history lives on your phone.** No account needed, every screen works offline; an optional
  account adds sync and restore across your own devices.
- **Snap it or type it.** Photograph a fuel receipt or type the entry – both are first-class doors,
  and a scan is a head start you correct.
- **The arithmetic, shown:** litres × price has to equal the total, on every entry.
- **Every powertrain and every currency:** petrol, diesel, hybrid and EV in one history; a fill-up
  abroad keeps both amounts at the rate of its own day.
- **Service entries, parts and reminders** alongside the fuel log.
- **Export is free and complete** – the receipt photos travel with the rows.
- **Import from Drivvo and My Fuel Manager**, review-first: nothing is written until you commit.
- **Recently deleted with a 30-day undo.**
