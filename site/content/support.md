## The fastest routes

**In the app: Settings → About & feedback → Send feedback.** The form sends straight from the app,
and it is the route that can attach the case you are writing about.

**By email: [to@belyaev.live](mailto:to@belyaev.live).** This is the support address – the same one
you will find on the App Store listing, so the two can never disagree. Mail reaches a human.

## What to include

The more specific the report, the faster the fix:

- what you did and what you expected;
- what happened instead – a screenshot of the screen helps;
- for a scan that read something wrong: the original photo, if you still have it. A misread receipt
  is a bug we want, and the photo is how we fix it;
- the app version (Settings → About).

Please do not send your whole log or receipts you would rather keep to yourself – export only what
you are comfortable sharing.

## Before writing

A scan that read a digit wrong is expected behaviour, not a broken app: the scan is a head start,
and you correct the rest. If a scan is consistently wrong for a format we should read well, that we
want to hear about.

## Status

Tankbook is not on the App Store yet – TestFlight is opening soon. Feedback during the test ring
shapes the release: write early, write often.
