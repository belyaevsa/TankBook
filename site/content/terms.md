These terms are short on purpose. They cover the Tankbook app and the optional services around it
(sync, restore, the extraction gateway, import parsing, feedback).

## What Tankbook is

Tankbook is a car cost log for iPhone. The app keeps your data in a database on your phone and
works fully without an account. Everything on your phone is yours; everything on our servers exists
only because you chose to sign in or to import a file.

## Accounts

An account is optional and exists for sync across your own devices, restore on a new phone, and the
cloud extraction gateway. You can delete your account at any time, from the app. Deletion removes
our copy of your data after a grace period; the log on your phone is not touched. See
[How to delete your account](/delete-account/) for exactly what happens.

## Your data

Your entries, photos and files are yours. We process them only to provide the services you asked
for – sync, restore, extraction, import parsing – and for nothing else. There are no ads, no
server-side analytics and no content analytics. Export is always free, in CSV or JSON.

## Fair use

Use Tankbook for your own records, do not abuse the services: no attempts to break access controls,
to exceed the published quotas, to probe other users' data, or to use the import or extraction
endpoints as a general-purpose processing service. We may suspend an account that endangers the
service for everyone else.

## Availability

We aim to keep the optional services running, but they are provided as is, without warranties of
any kind to the extent permitted by law. The app itself works offline and does not depend on our
servers; if the services disappear, your log stays with you and export keeps working.
